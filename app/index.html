Choose your AI

The app needs an AI to write questions and explain things. The first options work straight away: no account, no key.

Advanced: use your own free key (faster and more reliable)

Free keys from Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter make the app much faster. Your key stays in this browser only and is sent only to the service you pick.

Your work

Everything you do is saved automatically in this browser on this computer: your progress, question banks, assignments, drafts, notes and lessons.

The backup file is what you restore from. The .zip gives you one folder per module with its notes and assignments, to read or hand in.

Clearing your browser's history and site data would delete it, and it doesn't move to other computers by itself. Download a backup now and then; you can open it on any computer with Restore from a backup.

Delete everything

Removes all your progress, questions, assignments and your key from this browser.

Share with classmates and teachers

Send them this link. They just open it in Chrome. Nothing to install, no Terminal.

It works straight away with no account or key. Each person's work stays in their own browser, so nobody sees anyone else's work. Anyone who adds their own key keeps it to themselves.

Install it like an app

In Chrome, click the install icon at the right of the address bar (a screen with a down arrow), or the Install app button at the top of this page. It then opens in its own window from your Dock or Start menu.
